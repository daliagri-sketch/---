## GEMINI RAW STATUS

**RAW RESEARCH INPUT · UNVERIFIED · NOT SoT · NOT PRODUCT APPROVED · NOT FOR IMPLEMENTATION.**

Its Evidence Ledger is not accepted. Full claim-by-claim classification is the first substantive task after this review. Preliminary intake findings, so Main Control Tower can see the scale of the problem:

**1. Citation quality is weak and in places inconsistent.**
- Trauma-Informed UX rests on an addiction-recovery app development blog (IdeaUsher).
- Several "sources" are company accessibility statements, a cookie-banner blog, a Lovable course site and a web-design firm's legal page.
- Ledger entry for Fact vs Meaning cites "Tankelevitch et al., 2024" with arXiv `2606.02260`, which is a June 2026 identifier. Author, year and ID do not match.
- Cognitive-load entry gives DOI `10.3390/ijerph18168503` and `PMC10322198` as the same paper, and an author ("O. Waller") that needs checking.

**2. Several claims contradict closed Product truth. These are rejections, not research questions.**

| Gemini claim | Conflict |
|---|---|
| Parent deviation: "The system has updated the sequence based on this input" | Non-evidence day = same plan. Only Rule 5 can produce ADJUST |
| Insufficient evidence: "We need a few more days of practice data" | Implies a day count. Framework §3, §6: no counts |
| Insufficient evidence: "the system cannot proceed until more data is gathered" | KEEP(`insufficient_evidence`) continues practice. Nothing stops |
| KEEP framed as "positive stabilization" | KEEP reasons are `insufficient_evidence` / `mixed`. "Positive" creates false certainty |
| Non-evidence: "acknowledge effort without validating incorrect execution" | Frames the parent as wrong. Locked: no context echo, no verdict |
| "Cannot remember: provide Skip" | Required groups have UNKNOWN paths, not Skip (handoff v0.2 §4) |
| "Changed context: let the parent append context" | No generic context taxonomy. G2a is closed and template-specific |
| "No next target: celebrate" | Target closure does not auto-open a target; Planning approves. Heritage 12: recognition is not artificial praise |
| Safety stop copy "paused to ensure the foundation is secure" | Invents Safety meaning |
| First Plan as a "modifiable roadmap"; parent "selects" the target | Product logic not in any source. Parent changing the help sequence = V7, invalid |
| Part K: Check-in copy tone "SUPPORTED" | Gemini has no data to support it. At most NEEDS TESTING |

**3. "Potential conflicts" in Part K are mostly not conflicts.**
- 100vh / dvh: Morning CTA is in flow, never fixed. The risk Gemini describes applies to bottom-anchored CTAs. Residual question (Entry uses a full-height flex block) = TECHNICAL AUDIT REQUIRED.
- RTL via `row-reverse`: speculation. Release passed 06 Live Staging QA. Whether `dir="rtl"` and DOM order are correct = TECHNICAL AUDIT REQUIRED.
- G2 keyboard / screen reader: already specified (handoff v0.2 §7: fieldset / legend, arrow keys, `hidden`, `aria-live="polite"`, `aria-disabled` + `aria-describedby`). Verification = TECHNICAL AUDIT REQUIRED.

**4. Scope errors.**
- 24 × 24 CSS px (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8) is a floor. Morning uses 58 px buttons and rows ≥ 48 px. It is not a requirement for the Independence Target screen.
- 4.5:1 is the WCAG text-contrast threshold, not a "primary action" rule.
- Heebo / Assistant is a closed Product choice (with Karantina), not research.
- Alert tiering comes from prehospital paramedics. The product has no push notifications and Safety behaviour belongs to Safety SoT.

**5. Legal claims are outside Parent Experience authority.** SI 5568 version and WCAG mapping (Gemini contradicts itself: "aligns with WCAG AA" vs "based on 2.0 / 2.1"), Regulation 35 scope, the ~69,200 NIS figure and the widget claim all need primary-source or legal verification. 06 will record them as NEEDS SOURCE VERIFICATION and will not turn them into requirements.

**What looks usable after verification:** the Fact / Meaning separation, uncertainty visibility, recall-bias risk in retrospective reporting, native-semantics-first, and most of the Part L / Part O question list. Several of those already exist in project SoT (Framework §2, Heritage 4 and 8). Their authority will come from the project sources, not from Gemini.
